About me

From TVET college to streaming data

I’m a South African software engineer who enjoys turning messy, real-world processes into systems that work. My path into tech started at Coastal TVET College with the NC(V) in IT and Computer Science, carried on through a BSc in Information Technology at Richfield, and continues in the WeThinkCode_ Software Engineer programme.

Before tech I worked as a call centre agent, a cashier and a community volunteer. Those jobs taught me to listen to people, stay calm under pressure and keep the numbers accurate. I bring that into every system I build.

I care about clean design, tested code and being honest about trade-offs, and I’m looking for a team where I can keep shipping and keep learning.
